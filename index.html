Smart Shuttle

A shared commuting app that matches Cairo University students into shuttles or carpools along overlapping routes.

Problem
Students commuting independently to the same campus along overlapping routes means wasted cost, traffic, and emissions that shared transport could solve.
Solution
A geospatial matching engine — route proximity, radius/time-window filtering, and pickup sequencing — that groups compatible riders automatically.
My Role
Co-built with teammate Omar Mohamed. Owned the matching algorithm design and full system architecture (layered architecture, ER diagrams, UML suite).
Outcome
Working prototype delivered on deadline, advancing to the IntelliShift semi-final round.

CBE FinYology · Top Competitor

FinCircle

A bilingual Egyptian fintech platform built for the Central Bank of Egypt's national FinYology competition.

Problem
Egypt lacks accessible personal-finance tools that reflect local habits — Gam'eya savings circles, BNPL, Zakat — rather than a generic template.
Solution
A bilingual platform with Digital Gam'eya, a Smart BNPL advisor, an AI financial advisor that responds in Egyptian Arabic, EGX paper trading, NFC payments, and a Zakat calculator, built around an Egypt-first UX.
My Role
Led a 4-person team end-to-end: ideation, prototyping, business pitch, and live demo.
Outcome
Recognized as a top competitor in the CBE FinYology Hackathon.
