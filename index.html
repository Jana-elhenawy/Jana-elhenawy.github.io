A little about me

From writing code to building products

I started in Computer Science learning how to build software — then realized code is only part of a successful product.

The more projects I worked on, the more interested I became in understanding why something should be built, who it's for, and how it can solve a real problem.

Today, I'm a Computer Science student at Cairo University's Faculty of Computers and AI, a Full-Stack Developer, and a Product Owner Intern at Medicaspace, where I'm gaining hands-on experience turning ideas and business needs into practical digital solutions.

Along the way, I've built and shipped applications across fintech, fleet management, education, nutrition, and recipe discovery. My freelance experience has also taught me how to communicate with clients, understand requirements, scope projects, and turn those requirements into working products.

I don't just think about how to build a feature — I think about why it matters and how it should work for the people using it.

I'm currently deepening my expertise through DEPI's Full Stack .NET track, while continuing to grow at the intersection of software development and product thinking. My goal is simple: build digital products that are technically solid, useful, intuitive, and worth building.

Smart Shuttle

A shared commuting app that matches Cairo University students into shuttles or carpools along overlapping routes.

The Problem
Students commuting independently to the same campus along overlapping routes means wasted cost, traffic, and emissions that shared transport could solve.
The Solution
A geospatial matching engine — route proximity, radius/time-window filtering, and pickup sequencing — that groups compatible riders automatically.
My Role
Co-built with teammate Omar Mohamed. Owned the matching algorithm design and full system architecture (layered architecture, ER diagrams, UML suite).
Outcome
Working prototype delivered on deadline, advancing to the IntelliShift semi-final round.
